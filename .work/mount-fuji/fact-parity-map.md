## Numbers & Prices
- ¥4,000 — mandatory hiking fee per person, all trails, 2026 (was ¥2,000 mandatory + ¥1,000 optional in 2025)
- ¥3,800 — Shinjuku → Fuji-Subaru Line 5th Station bus one-way
- ¥8,000–¥15,000 — mountain hut per person per night
- ¥100–¥300 — toilet tip per use
- 4,000 — Yoshida Trail daily climber cap
- 3,776 m — summit elevation
- 0.6°C — temperature drop per 100 m gained
- 1.0°C — wind chill per 1 m/s wind speed
- 30L — recommended backpack capacity
- 1–2L — minimum water to carry

## Route Stats
- Yoshida: 6.8 km up (~6 hr), 7.0 km down (~3 hr), rock + gravel, most huts, most crowded
- Fujinomiya: 4.3 km up (~5 hr), 4.3 km down (~3 hr), shortest ascent, rocky, fewer huts
- Gotemba: 10.5 km up (~9 hr), 8.4 km down (~4 hr), volcanic gravel, longest, fewest huts, no first-aid station
- Subashiri: 6.9 km up (~7 hr), 6.2 km down (~4 hr), forest section then gravel, moderate huts

## Booking Rules
- Yoshida: Reserve via asoview (credit card/PayPay), free cancellation until day after payment; full charge from 2 days after
- Shizuoka trails: FUJI NAVI app, must pass e-learning + test, free cancellation until day of entry
- Mountain huts: book directly with each hut via phone or web (not through reservation system)
- On-site registration available but takes ~30 minutes (Shizuoka) and is subject to cap (Yoshida)

## Exceptions & Warnings
- Bullet climbing (overnight ascent without hut stay) PROHIBITED on all trails
- Children pay same ¥4,000 fee; no child discount
- No refunds for Yoshida reservation after 2 days post-payment
- No daily limit on Shizuoka trails
- No first-aid center on Gotemba Trail
- No running water or baths at mountain huts
- No private vehicles during restriction periods (shuttle bus/taxi only)
- Heavy rain: Mt. Fuji Skyline closes, no shuttle alternative
- No pets allowed on trails
- No drones
- Carry out all trash (no bins on mountain)
- Summit temperature near 0°C even in summer
- Emergency: 110 (police, above 6th station), 119 (fire/ambulance, near 5th station)

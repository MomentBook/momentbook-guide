# Reader-Decision Brief: Mount Fuji Climbing Guide 2026

**Traveler**: International traveler (non-Japanese speaker, first-time Japan visitor or returning visitor) planning to climb Mount Fuji during the 2026 summer season.

**Decision the article helps them make**: Which of the four official trails to take, whether to book a mountain hut or attempt a single-day climb, how to pre-register and pay the ¥4,000 hiking fee, and what gear will pass the gate check.

**Main constraint**: The climbing window is only 71 days (July 1–September 10, 2026 for Yoshida/Subashiri; July 10–September 10 for Fujinomiya/Gotemba). Yoshida Trail has a hard 4,000-climber daily cap with gate closures. Bullet climbing (overnight ascent without a hut stay) is prohibited on all trails. Mountain huts fill early.

**Likely wrong assumption**: "I can just show up, pay cash at the gate, and climb through the night to see sunrise."

**Volatile facts to recheck**:
- 2027 hiking fee (¥4,000 in 2026; was ¥2,000 in 2025)
- 2027 season dates (typically similar but shift by day of week)
- Daily climber cap thresholds (Yoshida 4,000 as of 2026)
- Bus schedules and fares from Tokyo
- Mountain hut availability and pricing
- Volcano alert level (check before climbing)
- Private vehicle restriction dates

## Verdict

PASS.

The topic is supportable from official sources, with one important framing constraint: Arubus supports airport access on Line 2 and hotel-area service from Oranjestad on Line 10 variants, so airport-to-hotel-area guidance should be written as an airport-to-Oranjestad/hotel-area transfer pattern unless a future official source confirms a direct airport-to-hotel-strip bus.

## Hard facts allowed

- ZimParks places Victoria Falls National Park in northwestern Zimbabwe.
- ZimParks states the waterfall is 108 m high and 1,708 m wide.
- ZimParks states Victoria Falls was added to the UNESCO World Heritage List in 1989.
- ZimParks states the park covers about 23.4 sq km and the rainforest is watered by spray from the Falls.
- ZimParks 2026 tariff PDF states charges are inclusive of VAT.
- ZimParks 2026 Rainforest Main Gate daily conservation fee: local day visitor US$7, SADC day visitor US$35, international day visitor US$58.
- ZimParks 2026 Rainforest VIP Gate fee: local US$30, SADC US$87, international US$174.
- ZimParks 2026 Rainforest Moonlight Viewing fee: local US$15, SADC US$81, international US$116.
- Zimbabwe Tourism Authority says February to May is the best time for the roaring falls after summer rains.
- Zimbabwe Tourism Authority advises sightseers to take an umbrella because of spray.
